# WatchTower

Upstate New York, watched from one place: deal flow, news, politics, business, faith communities and history across four
regions — Buffalo/Niagara & West, Rochester & Area, Central NY, and Albany & North.

- **Live app:** https://claude.ai/artifact/KH8bkJabzjnkJnCVFALYfr (private until shared from its Share menu)
- **Money in Motion:** M&A, capital projects, philanthropy and commercial real estate, each with the named sellers or donors.
- **Briefing:** dated government, business and faith news with sources.
- **Politics & Officials:** Congress, state legislature, county and city leaders, town supervisors and judges, with party and results.
- **Business, Faith, History:** regional employers and industries, religious and cultural communities, and regional history.

## Working on it
- Content: `app/data.js`. Layout: `app/app_template.html`. Build: `python3 build.py` → `dist/watchtower.html`.
- Rules for every session: `CLAUDE.md`. Weekly update procedure: `REFRESH_INSTRUCTIONS.md`. History: `CHANGELOG.md`.

Public information only; every item links to its source.
